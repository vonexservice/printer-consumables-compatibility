# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve this resource. Accuracy matters more than the number of records.

## Before submitting a record

- Confirm the exact printer model, including suffixes and regional variants.
- Confirm the exact cartridge or consumable part number.
- Use a reliable source, preferably the printer or consumable manufacturer's documentation.
- Record the region when compatibility differs by market.
- Include yield only when a reliable source publishes it, and preserve the stated yield standard.
- Identify original/OEM, compatible, and remanufactured products accurately.

## Please do not submit

- Guessed matches based on similar model names.
- Bulk scraped or copyrighted catalog data without permission.
- Links created only to promote a business or product.
- Hidden advertising, keyword-stuffed descriptions, or unverifiable claims.
- Private customer information or supplier credentials.

## How to propose a change

Open an issue or pull request and include:

1. Exact printer model.
2. Exact consumable part number and type.
3. Region, if relevant.
4. Direct source URL and source title.
5. Date checked.
6. Any limitations or conflicting evidence.

If a source is unavailable or ambiguous, set the status to `unverified` or `needs_review`. Do not silently promote it to verified.

## Corrections

Please explain what is incorrect, link to the better source, and identify affected rows. Corrections are welcome; the goal is a resource that others can audit and trust.
