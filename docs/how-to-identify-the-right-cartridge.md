# How to identify the right printer cartridge

Use this checklist before ordering ink, toner, drums, or maintenance supplies.

## 1. Find the exact printer model

Look on the front or top of the printer, on the label at the back, or in the printer's settings/status report. Record the complete model name, including letters at the end (such as DW, DN, or CDW). Similar model numbers do not guarantee the same consumables.

## 2. Check the cartridge number

If a cartridge is already installed, use its label as a clue, but confirm the number in the printer manufacturer's manual or official supplies page. A cartridge number may have regional variations, and a drum unit is not the same thing as a toner cartridge.

## 3. Match the product type

Confirm each detail before purchasing:

- Exact printer model and regional version
- Cartridge part number
- Colour (black, cyan, magenta, yellow, or multipack)
- Original/OEM, compatible, or remanufactured status
- Standard or high-yield version
- Whether the printer uses a separate drum, waste container, or maintenance kit

## 4. Understand page yield

Published yields are estimates based on stated test methods and page coverage. Compare yields only when the test standard and product type are comparable. Real-world yield varies with document coverage, print settings, and use.

## 5. Verify the region

Do not assume a part number listed on a US page is automatically the correct Canadian SKU. Check the Canadian manufacturer page or documentation for your exact model before ordering.

## 6. When sources disagree

Pause rather than guess. Check the printer's manual, the manufacturer's official consumables page, or contact manufacturer support. Keep uncertain entries marked as `needs_review` in this project.
